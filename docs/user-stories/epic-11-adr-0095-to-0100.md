Stories derived from the ADRs drafted in `docs/adr/`. Backend stories are sequenced before their frontend counterparts where both repos are touched.

## Story 11.1 — CRM connector and case handoff (backend)

**Source:** ADR-0095 (Accepted 2026-08-28) · **Status:** Ready
**Built:** 2026-08-28 — social-listening-core

**As a** backend engineer,
**I want** a `CRMConnector` provider abstraction (supporting Microsoft Dynamics 365, Salesforce, and HubSpot), `crm_field_mappings` table, and `POST /v1/inbox/items/:id/case`,
**so that** `Tenant-Social-Care-Agent` and `Social-Selling-Strategist` can escalate inbox items, posts, and prospective leads to external CRMs.

**Acceptance Criteria**
- `CRMConnector` interface with `pushEntity()`, `validateCredentials()`, and `status()`.
- Supports `CRMProviderType` (`dynamics365`, `salesforce`, `hubspot`) and `CRMEntityType` (`lead`, `opportunity`, `support`).
- Microsoft Dynamics 365 Dataverse Web API v9.2 integration with Azure AD OAuth authentication and canonical deep links.
- Generalized `CRMCasePayload` supporting both post-level escalation and author-only prospecting handoffs (ADR-0086/0117).
- CRM credentials stored in `platform_credentials` with `credential_type='crm'`.
- `crm_field_mappings` table with tenant RLS lets `Tenant-Admin` override default field mappings.
- `POST /v1/inbox/items/:id/case` accepts `crmConnectorId`, `entityType`, `assignedTo`, `notes`, `customFields`, and `allowDuplicate`.
- Returns `outboundActivityId`, `crmRecordId`, and `crmRecordUrl`.
- Fail-closed deduplication returns `409 Conflict` if the item was already successfully pushed to the connector, unless `allowDuplicate: true` is passed.
- Every push attempt records an `outbound_activities` row (`activity_type='crm_handoff'`) with `status` and `error_details` on failure.

## Story 11.2 — Case handoff to CRM UI (frontend)

**Source:** ADR-0095 (Accepted 2026-08-28) · **Status:** Ready
**Built:** 2026-08-28 — social-listening-admin

**As a** `Tenant-Social-Care-Agent` or `Social-Selling-Strategist`,
**I want** a "Create Case / Lead in CRM" action on post detail, inbox, and prospecting lists,
**so that** I can push relevant social interactions and leads into our CRM without leaving the platform.

**Acceptance Criteria**
- "Create in CRM" button on `PostDetail`, `InboxItem`, and prospecting list author rows.
- Modal lets the user select the active CRM connector (Dynamics 365, Salesforce, HubSpot), `entityType` (`lead`, `opportunity`, `support`), assignee, and optional notes.
- On duplicate attempt without override, surfaces `409 Conflict` notice with direct link to existing CRM record and option to proceed.
- On success, displays the generated deep link to open the case/lead directly in the target CRM portal.
- Inline error reporting with retry actions for failed handoffs.

## Story 11.3 — Daily digest email (backend)

**Source:** ADR-0096 · **Status:** Ready
**Built:** not yet

**As a** backend engineer,
**I want** `user_digest_preferences`, an hourly digest scheduler, and a rendered email,
**so that** `Tenant-Reader` receives a daily summary of watchlist activity.

**Acceptance Criteria**
- `user_digest_preferences` table with `send_at_utc`, `timezone`, `watchlist_ids`, and `include_*` flags.
- Hourly worker checks for users whose local send time is due.
- Digest content uses `*DailyCount` tables (ADR-0087) for fast aggregation.
- `user_digest_preferences` table includes `is_enabled`, `send_at_utc`, `timezone`, `watchlist_ids`, `include_ai_summary`, `include_top_posts`, `include_topic_breakdown`, and `last_sent_at`.
- Hourly worker only sends when local `send_at_utc` has passed and `last_sent_at` is older than 20 hours.
- Digest content uses `*DailyCount` tables (ADR-0087) for `totalMentions`, `sentiment`, `topTopics`, and `topPlatforms`.
- `notablePosts` are capped at 5 and `aiSummary` is bounded to one `AIProviderConnector.research()` call per user per day.
- Email subject is `"<tenant> daily digest — <date>"` with plain-text and HTML bodies.
- `POST /v1/users/me/digest-preferences` configures preferences; `POST /v1/users/me/digest-previews` returns a one-off preview.

## Story 11.5 — Topic evolution timeline (backend)

**Source:** ADR-0097 · **Status:** Ready
**Built:** not yet

**As a** backend engineer,
**I want** `GET /v1/topics/evolution` to return time-series data for a topic's volume, sentiment, top authors, and keywords,
**so that** `Topic-Center-Analyst` can see how a conversation developed.

**Acceptance Criteria**
- Endpoint accepts `topicId`, `start`, `end`, and `granularity` (`day`, `week`, `month`).
- Data sources: `TopicDailyCount`, `SentimentDailyCount`, `AuthorTopicSignal`, and `post_topics`.
- Response includes `points` with `mentionCount`, `uniqueAuthors`, `sentiment`, `topAuthors`, `topKeywords`, and `trend`.
- `trend` is computed from the 7-day slope of `mentionCount`: `rising` if slope > 5% per day, `falling` if < -5% per day, otherwise `stable`.
- Response includes `topicId`, `topicName`, `startDate`, `endDate`, `granularity`, and `points`.
- Supports `compareToPrevious` for overlaying the previous period.

## Story 11.7 — Publishing and scheduling (backend)

**Source:** ADR-0098 · **Status:** Ready
**Built:** not yet

**As a** backend engineer,
**I want** `SocialConnector.publish?()`, scheduled `outbound_activities`, and `POST /v1/outbound/posts`,
**so that** the composer can publish real posts to connected platforms.

**Acceptance Criteria**
- `SocialConnector` exposes optional `publish?()` with `OutboundPost` and returns `platformPostId` and `url`.
- `outbound_activities` supports `activity_type='post'` with `status` values `scheduled`, `publishing`, `published`, `failed`, or `cancelled`, plus `scheduled_for` and `published_at`.
- `POST /v1/outbound/posts` creates one activity per target platform, supports `scheduledFor`, and returns `202` with `activityIds` and `scheduledFor`.
- A scheduler worker dispatches scheduled posts at `scheduled_for`.
- `PATCH .../cancel` and `PATCH .../reschedule` endpoints are available.
- `GET /v1/connectors/:platformId/targets` lists available pages/accounts for an asset-targeting platform.
- Requests without a required `assetTargets` entry return `400 MISSING_ASSET_TARGET`.
- At least one platform (e.g. LinkedIn or Bluesky) implements `publish?()`.

## Story 11.9 — Unified social inbox and reply (backend)

**Source:** ADR-0099 · **Status:** Ready
**Built:** not yet

**As a** backend engineer,
**I want** `inbox_items`, triage state, and `POST /v1/inbox/:id/reply`,
**so that** `Tenant-Social-Care-Agent` can manage and respond to matched posts.

**Acceptance Criteria**
- `inbox_items` table with `priority`, `status`, `assigned_to`, `snoozed_until`, `notes`, and `tags`.
- `GET /v1/inbox` supports filters for status, priority, assigned user, watchlist, and platform.
- `PATCH` and assignment endpoints are RLS-scoped.
- `POST /v1/inbox/:id/reply` proxies to the reply endpoint (ADR-0073) and marks the item `resolved` on success.
- Inbox items are created from `IngestionPostMatchedEvent`.
- `priority` defaults to `urgent`/`high`/`normal` from sentiment and author reach rules.
- `POST /v1/inbox/:id/assign`, `POST /v1/inbox/:id/snooze`, and `POST /v1/inbox/:id/resolve` endpoints are RLS-scoped.
- Redacted posts automatically mark their `inbox_items` as `resolved` with `notes='redacted'`.

## Story 11.11 — Composed post author mention suggestions (backend)

**Source:** ADR-0100 · **Status:** Ready (depends on Stories 9.10 and 11.10)
**Built:** not yet

**As a** backend engineer,
**I want** `POST /v1/composer/mention-suggestions` to combine `AuthorTopicSignal`, `RAG` search, and keyword signals,
**so that** the composer can suggest relevant authors to mention.

**Acceptance Criteria**
- Endpoint accepts `text`, `targetPlatforms`, `watchlistId`, and `maxSuggestions`.
- Suggestions include `authorId`, `authorName`, `platformId`, `handle`, `reason`, `matchSource`, and `confidence`.
- `AuthorTopicSignal`, `RAG` search, and keyword match are combined with weights.
- Authors already mentioned in the draft are excluded.
- Only authors active on the selected `targetPlatforms` are returned.
- `maxSuggestions` defaults to 5 and is hard-capped at 10.
- Suggestions are ranked by a weighted combination with `AuthorTopicSignal` weighted highest, and duplicates across platforms are merged.
