**As an** author / data subject,  
**I want** public bot-protected rights submission and statutory 45-day SLA tracking,  
**so that** my takedown request is verified, reliably tracked, and cascades into derived AI enrichment data upon grant.

**Acceptance Criteria**
- `POST /public/v1/takedowns` enforces mandatory bot challenge token (`captchaToken`) returning 400 `CAPTCHA_VERIFICATION_FAILED` if omitted or invalid.
- `POST /public/v1/takedowns/verify` initializes statutory `sla_due_at` clock to `created_at + 45 days` upon magic-link verification.
- `GET /v1/takedowns` surfaces advisory `risk_flag` and `risk_reason` to reviewers while strictly preserving human-in-the-loop decision rule (automated grants or denials are blocked with 403 `AUTO_DECISION_FORBIDDEN`).
- `POST /v1/takedowns/:id/grant` soft-redacts post body (`[Redacted per Data Subject Request]`) and raw payload (`{ redacted: true }`), clears derived AI enrichment (`sentiment`, `sentimentConfidence`, `keyPhrases`, `topicClusters`), removes watchlist matches (`post_watchlist_matches`), and synchronously purges RAG vector chunks via `RAGConnector.deletePost()`.
- Rejection or denial via `POST /v1/takedowns/:id/deny` requires mandatory legal reason notes logged in `data_subject_requests` audit trail.

## Story 16.2 — DSR Article 18 restriction quarantining (backend)

**Source:** ADR-0126 (Accepted 2026-08-28) · **Status:** Built  
**Built:** 2026-09-08 — social-listening-core@7336ec5  
*(Relates to Story 10.12 — see epic-10 for foundation. **Documentation Steward correction, 2026-09-13:** previously said "Story 10.13" — ADR-0126 (this story's own Source) is a refinement of ADR-0093 (DSR self-service portal), which is Story 10.12, not 10.13 (compliance audit pack, ADR-0094). Also note, same as Story 16.1 above: Story 10.12's own documentation now shows it was never actually built as originally scoped — this Story 16.2 commit's real diff (`dsr_receipts`, Article 18 quarantine routes, migration `0078`) is the first real DSR implementation, not a refinement of prior work. **As a** data subject / contesting author,  
**I want** to exercise my GDPR Article 18 right to restrict data processing and receive a verifiable digital receipt,  
**so that** contested social content is immediately quarantined from analytics, exports, and AI search without premature permanent deletion, and my submission is backed by cryptographic proof.

**Acceptance Criteria**
- `POST /public/v1/dsr/requests` supports `restriction` (Article 18) request type alongside `access`, `rectification`, and `erasure`, enforcing bot verification (`captchaToken`) and IP rate limiting.
- Submitting a verified restriction request generates a cryptographically signed HMAC-SHA256 receipt containing `{ requestId, tenantId, subjectHash, requestType, timestamp }`, persisted in `dsr_receipts` and returned in the HTTP 201 response.
- `GET /public/v1/dsr/verify-receipt` accepts receipt tokens or payloads to publicly validate signature authenticity using constant-time verification (`crypto.timingSafeEqual`) without exposing personal identity.
- `POST /v1/dsr/requests/:id/quarantine` sets `social_posts.processing_restricted = TRUE` for targeted posts, with reverse remediation via `POST /v1/dsr/requests/:id/unquarantine` restricted to `Tenant-Admin` and `Legal-Advisor`.
- Contested posts with `processing_restricted = TRUE` are excluded from:
  1. Analytics aggregations (`GET /v1/analytics/overview`, time-series volumes, sentiment scores).
  2. Data export generation (`GET /v1/posts/export.csv` and JSON export streams).
  3. RAG vector search retrieval queries (`WHERE processing_restricted = FALSE`).
- Quarantined records preserve raw payload data and relational integrity under partial index `idx_social_posts_active_processing` pending legal determination.

## Story 16.3 — Cryptographic audit log hash chaining and manifest export (backend)

**Source:** ADR-0127 (Accepted 2026-08-28) · **Status:** Built  
**Built:** 2026-09-08 — social-listening-core@492bbd1  
*(Relates to Story 10.13 — see epic-10 for foundation. **Documentation Steward correction, 2026-09-13:** previously said "Story 10.14 and Story 10.15" — Story 10.15 does not exist anywhere in `docs/user-stories/epic-10-adr-0086-to-0094.md` (that file ends at Story 10.14), and ADR-0127 (this story's own Source) is a refinement of ADR-0094 (compliance audit pack), which is Story 10.13, not 10.14 (trust and rights admin UI, a frontend story). Also note, same as Stories 16.1/16.2 above: Story 10.13's own documentation now shows it was never actually built as originally scoped — this Story 16.3 commit's real diff (`compliance_evidence_links`, audit hash chaining, migration `0079`) is the first real compliance-audit-pack implementation, not a refinement of prior work. Flagged for Menno to formally reconcile.*)

**As a** compliance officer / platform administrator,  
**I want** audit log entries to maintain cryptographic hash chaining and export verifiable compliance evidence bundles with a signed Merkle-root manifest,  
**so that** regulatory audits (SOC 2, ISO 27001, GDPR Article 30) can mathematically prove the chronological sequence, tamper-evident immutability, and completeness of administrative operations.

**Acceptance Criteria**
- Audit log tables (`platform_admin_audit_log` and tenant audit tables) store `previous_record_hash TEXT NOT NULL` and `record_hash TEXT NOT NULL`.
- Hash computation deterministically computes `record_hash = SHA256(id | tenant_id | actor_id | action | timestamp | canonical_payload | previous_record_hash)`, with the genesis block anchored to 64 zeros (`0000...0000`).
- Row-level database locking (`FOR UPDATE` on the latest sequence record) prevents concurrency race conditions during chained sequential appends.
- `GET /v1/compliance/audit-log/verify` sequentially re-computes and verifies the hash chain across any requested time window or tenant scope, returning `isValid: true` or pinpointing the exact compromised row ID, sequence position, and mismatched digest.
- `POST /v1/compliance/audit-packs` packages audit records, DSR proofs, and exports into a structured ZIP archive featuring a root `manifest.json`.
- `manifest.json` conforms to schema `manifestVersion: "1.0.0"` containing `packId`, `tenantId`, `generatedAt`, `timeRange`, file digests (`path`, `sha256`, `rowCount`, `byteSize`), Merkle root hash of all enclosed records, and a platform cryptographic verification signature.
- Presigned download URLs via `GET /v1/compliance/audit-packs/:id/download` enforce a 24-hour expiration window with 90-day storage lifecycle retention.

## Story 16.4 — Platform ops quota burn-rate forecasting (frontend/backend)

**Source:** ADR-0128 (Accepted 2026-08-28) · **Status:** Built  
**Built:** 2026-09-08 (commit `48d2f8f`)  
*(Relates to Story 10.7 and Story 13.8 — see epic-10 and epic-13 for foundation)*

**As a** platform operator / platform administrator,  
**I want** tenant quota velocity burn-rate forecasting and guided connector remediation controls in the platform operations dashboard,  
**so that** I can anticipate monthly quota/token exhaustion before service interruptions occur and resolve connector faults in under 60 seconds without manual terminal intervention.

**Acceptance Criteria**
- Backend computes trailing 7-day token/ingestion velocity and linear burn-rate projection:
  $$\text{dailyVelocity7d} = \frac{\sum_{i=1}^7 \text{Tokens}_i}{7}, \quad \text{daysRemaining} = \frac{\text{remainingQuota}}{\text{dailyVelocity7d}}$$
  classifying status as `healthy` (>30d remaining), `warning_30d` (8–30d remaining), or `critical_7d` (≤7d remaining or exhausted).
- `GET /v1/admin/platform-dashboard` (and Admin BFF `/api/admin/platform-dashboard`) returns `tenantQuotaBurnProjections` containing `tenantId`, `tenantName`, `monthlyQuota`, `consumedTokens`, `dailyVelocity7d`, `projectedExhaustionDate`, and `status`.
- The Platform Operations UI (`/admin/operations`) renders a `QuotaBurnRateForecast` widget with exhaustion timeline projections, warning thresholds, and visual risk indicators.
- `POST /v1/admin/connectors/:id/remediate` provides role-gated (`platform_admin`) remediation playbooks: `retry_now`, `reprompt_credentials`, `override_backoff` (with `overrideMinutes`), and `clear_error_state`.
- An interactive `ConnectorRemediationDrawer` component allows platform operators to execute remediation playbooks directly from stalled connector cards, providing real-time feedback and logging all actions to `platform_admin_audit_log`.
