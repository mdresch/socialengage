## Story 16.1 — Author-initiated takedown SLA tracking and enrichment cascade (backend)

**Source:** ADR-0125 (Accepted 2026-08-28) · **Status:** Ready  
**Built:** 2026-09-08 — social-listening-core@689357c  
*(Relates to Story 10.11 — see epic-10 for foundation)*

**As an** author / data subject,  
**I want** public bot-protected rights submission and statutory 45-day SLA tracking,  
**so that** my takedown request is verified, reliably tracked, and cascades into derived AI enrichment data upon grant.

**Acceptance Criteria**
- `POST /public/v1/takedowns` enforces mandatory bot challenge token (`captchaToken`) returning 400 `CAPTCHA_VERIFICATION_FAILED` if omitted or invalid.
- `POST /public/v1/takedowns/verify` initializes statutory `sla_due_at` clock to `created_at + 45 days` upon magic-link verification.
- `GET /v1/takedowns` surfaces advisory `risk_flag` and `risk_reason` to reviewers while strictly preserving human-in-the-loop decision rule (auto-resolve blocked with 403 `AUTO_DECISION_FORBIDDEN`).
- `POST /v1/takedowns/:id/grant` soft-redacts post body and raw payload, clears derived AI enrichment (`sentiment`, `sentimentConfidence`, `keyPhrases`, `topicClusters`), removes watchlist matches, and synchronously purges RAG vector chunks.
