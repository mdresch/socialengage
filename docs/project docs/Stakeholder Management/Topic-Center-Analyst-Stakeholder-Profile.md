# Topic-Center-Analyst Stakeholder Profile

## Purpose

This profile captures the requirements and concerns of the Topic Center Analyst stakeholder for the Social Listening / Insights subsystem. It is intended to support requirements elicitation, story drafting, and design review by making this future role's needs explicit without implying that a real topic-center capability exists yet in the product.

## Stakeholder Type

- Role: Topic Center Analyst
- Category: Future internal stakeholder (tenant-scoped research and trend analysis role)
- Status: Not yet instantiated in production code; represented here as a requirements-facing persona for design and delivery planning
- Source basis: the 2026-08-05 stakeholder register addendum, the future Topic Center concept, and the project's existing topic and trend-analysis concepts

## Core Responsibilities

A Topic Center Analyst would investigate emerging themes, topic evolution, and trend trajectories. This includes:

- reviewing topics and their related activity over time
- identifying emerging or accelerating themes
- detecting semantic drift or changing language around a topic
- comparing topic-level activity across sources or time periods
- exploring the authors and conversations that contribute to a topic
- using the platform to inform research and strategic analysis

## Primary Goals

A Topic Center Analyst wants to:

- understand how topics evolve over time
- distinguish meaningful trend shifts from short-lived noise
- investigate topic-level patterns with clarity and confidence
- connect topic analysis to broader business or communications context
- reduce the effort needed to move from raw conversation data to useful insight

## Key Concerns

### Trend interpretation

The Topic Center Analyst cares most about whether the platform can show how topics are changing, where attention is moving, and whether a pattern appears to be sustained or temporary.

### Analytical depth

The stakeholder needs enough context and flexibility to investigate topics beyond simple volume counts, including related authors, sources, sentiment, and temporal change.

### Usability for research

The experience should support exploratory analysis without forcing the analyst to rely on technical queries or manual aggregation.

### Trust in interpretation

The stakeholder needs confidence that the topic summaries and trend views are grounded in the underlying data and are not misleading because of skew, noise, or incomplete coverage.

## Requirements Implications

The Topic Center Analyst stakeholder implies the following product expectations:

- the platform should support topic-level analysis, trend tracking, and research-oriented exploration
- topic views should be clear, flexible, and suitable for analytical use
- users should be able to investigate related authors, sources, and conversation context
- the system should support comparison across time periods and content dimensions
- the experience should help turn raw conversation data into structured insight

## Suggested Use in Requirements Work

Use this profile when drafting or reviewing:

- topic-center and trend-analysis workflows
- topic exploration and semantic-drift requirements
- author-topic and source-topic analysis needs
- research-oriented analytics and reporting requirements

## Notes

This profile is a design-time stakeholder artifact, not a substitute for real user validation. It should be used to broaden coverage during requirements work, especially where no live Topic Center Analyst currently exists in the system.
