# Social-Selling-Strategist Stakeholder Profile

## Purpose

This profile captures the requirements and concerns of the Social Selling Strategist stakeholder for the Social Listening / Insights subsystem. It is intended to support requirements elicitation, story drafting, and design review by making this future role's needs explicit without implying that a real social-selling capability exists yet in the product.

## Stakeholder Type

- Role: Social Selling Strategist
- Category: Future internal stakeholder (tenant-scoped growth and prospecting role)
- Status: Not yet instantiated in production code; represented here as a requirements-facing persona for design and delivery planning
- Source basis: the 2026-08-05 stakeholder register addendum, the future Social Selling subsystem concept, and the project's existing author and topic signal concepts

## Core Responsibilities

A Social Selling Strategist would identify opportunities for relationship-building and lead generation based on observed social activity. This includes:

- identifying experts, advocates, or relevant authors
- finding conversations that indicate potential commercial interest or relevance
- reviewing topic and author signals to establish fit
- prioritizing targets or opportunities based on relevance and engagement
- using the platform to support prospecting and relationship development workflows

## Primary Goals

A Social Selling Strategist wants to:

- find relevant people and conversations efficiently
- understand which topics or authors are most relevant to their goals
- trust that the platform surfaces actionable opportunities
- move from discovery to relationship-building with minimal friction
- avoid spending time on low-value or irrelevant signals

## Key Concerns

### Relevance

The Social Selling Strategist cares most about whether the platform can surface the right people, topics, and conversations for prospecting and relationship-building.

### Signal quality

The stakeholder needs confidence that the data reflects genuine relevance rather than generic activity or noise.

### Discoverability

The experience should make it easy to explore authors, topics, and related conversations without needing deep technical knowledge.

### Efficiency

The stakeholder values workflows that reduce time spent filtering through large volumes of social content to find worthwhile opportunities.

## Requirements Implications

The Social Selling Strategist stakeholder implies the following product expectations:

- the platform should support discovery of relevant authors and topics
- author and topic views should be usable for prospecting-style analysis
- filtering and prioritization features should help identify meaningful signals
- the experience should support workflows that connect insight to relationship-building activity
- the system should make it easy to distinguish high-value opportunities from background noise

## Suggested Use in Requirements Work

Use this profile when drafting or reviewing:

- author and topic discovery workflows
- prospecting and relationship-building requirements
- relevance filtering and prioritization needs
- growth-oriented analytics views

## Notes

This profile is a design-time stakeholder artifact, not a substitute for real user validation. It should be used to broaden coverage during requirements work, especially where no live Social Selling Strategist currently exists in the system.
