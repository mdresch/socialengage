# Tenant-Social-Care-Agent Stakeholder Profile

## Purpose

This profile captures the requirements and concerns of the Tenant Social Care Agent stakeholder for the Social Listening / Insights subsystem. It is intended to support requirements elicitation, story drafting, and design review by making this future role's needs explicit without implying that a real social-care workflow exists yet in the product.

## Stakeholder Type

- Role: Tenant Social Care Agent
- Category: Future internal stakeholder (tenant-scoped support and care role)
- Status: Not yet instantiated in production code; represented here as a requirements-facing persona for design and delivery planning
- Source basis: the 2026-08-05 stakeholder register addendum, the future Social Care subsystem concept, and the project's existing post-response and escalation concepts

## Core Responsibilities

A Tenant Social Care Agent would respond to customer issues and questions surfaced through social conversations. This includes:

- identifying customer-facing posts that require attention
- understanding the context of a complaint, question, or issue
- prioritizing outreach based on urgency or impact
- reviewing conversations and associated metadata before responding
- coordinating with internal or tenant stakeholders when escalation is necessary
- using the platform to support service recovery and relationship management

## Primary Goals

A Tenant Social Care Agent wants to:

- resolve customer issues quickly and consistently
- understand the situation behind a post without unnecessary friction
- act with confidence and appropriate context
- avoid missing important complaints or recurring issues
- maintain a clear workflow from detection to response

## Key Concerns

### Timeliness

The Tenant Social Care Agent cares most about whether the platform surfaces emerging complaints or service issues quickly enough to support a timely response.

### Context and ownership

The stakeholder needs enough detail to understand the issue, the source, and the appropriate response path without needing to search across multiple tools.

### Consistency

The workflow should support a consistent and controlled response process so that customer care actions are handled reliably and with appropriate oversight.

### Escalation readiness

The stakeholder needs the ability to identify when an issue should be escalated to a more senior role, another team, or a CRM-style workflow when such integration is later supported.

## Requirements Implications

The Tenant Social Care Agent stakeholder implies the following product expectations:

- the platform should support social-care-oriented triage and response workflows
- conversations should be easy to review in context and prioritize by urgency
- customer-facing issues should be visible and actionable
- the system should support a clear handoff or escalation path where appropriate
- the experience should help reduce missed issues and inconsistent handling

## Suggested Use in Requirements Work

Use this profile when drafting or reviewing:

- support and response workflows
- conversation prioritization and triage requirements
- escalation and case-handling patterns
- customer-care-oriented UI and workflow design

## Notes

This profile is a design-time stakeholder artifact, not a substitute for real user validation. It should be used to broaden coverage during requirements work, especially where no live Social Care Agent currently exists in the system.
