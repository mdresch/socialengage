# Watchlist management screen

## Story
Story 6.4 — Watchlist management screen

## Intent
Provide a tenant-facing screen for listing, creating, editing, and deleting watchlists without requiring direct API calls.

## Governing decisions
- Story 1.5 watchlist CRUD surface in social-listening-core.
- The UI should support keyword, hashtag, account, and boolean watchlists.

## Notes
- The screen should offer a simple create/edit form and a confirm step for delete actions.
- The tenant-facing shell should expose the management experience to tenant users and tenant admins.
